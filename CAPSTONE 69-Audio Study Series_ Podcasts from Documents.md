# AUDIO STUDY SERIES

## Computer Networks Podcasts from Documents

## 1. Project Overview

The Audio Study Series is an AI-powered educational project that
converts Computer Networks study materials into audio podcasts. It helps
students understand networking concepts through audio learning instead
of reading lengthy study materials.

## 2. Objectives

-   Convert Computer Networks documents into audio podcasts.
-   Make learning easier through clear audio explanations.
-   Verify the accuracy of AI-generated information.
-   Compare learning performance before and after listening to the
    podcasts.
-   Provide a quiz to evaluate students' understanding.

## 3. Technologies and AI Tools

  Tool           Purpose
  -------------- ------------------------------------------------------
  NotebookLM     Generate audio overviews from source documents.
  ChatGPT        Support research, summarisation and quiz creation.
  Gemini         Assist with research and content verification.
  Gamma          Create the project presentation.
  Google Drive   Store and share audio files and project resources.
  GitHub         Host project documentation and submission materials.

## 4. Topics Covered

1.  OSI Model and TCP/IP Models
2.  IP Addressing and Subnetting
3.  Routing and Switching
4.  Network Security

## 5. Project Workflow

1.  Collect Computer Networks study documents from reliable and
    permitted sources.
2.  Review the source materials and verify that they are suitable for
    the project.
3.  Upload the selected documents into an AI audio-generation tool.
4.  Generate four audio podcasts, one for each selected topic.
5.  Listen to each generated podcast and fact-check its content against
    the original source.
6.  Prepare a learning test and a quiz to assess understanding.
7.  Compare the learning results before and after listening to the
    podcasts.
8.  Prepare the final presentation and demonstration.

This project demonstrates how Artificial Intelligence can support

engaging way to learn Computer Networks. The usefulness of the podcasts
will be evaluated through fact-checking and learning assessments.
